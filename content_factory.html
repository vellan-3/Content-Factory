What makes a reply slap
First 5 words
Must stop the scroll. No "great post!" No agreeing immediately. Start mid-thought or with a counter-frame.
Length
1–2 sentences max. The shorter the more confident it reads. Exceptions: value-add replies can be 3–4 sentences.
No hedging
Never "I think", "imo", "maybe". Say the thing. Confidence = engagement. Doubt = scroll past.
Make them reply
Every reply should contain something slightly off, incomplete, or provocative enough that the OP or thread has to respond.
Meme energy
Emoji at the right place = emotional punctuation. Not decoration. 1–2 max. Don't announce the joke.
Timing
On a banger: reply within the first hour. Your reply rides the wave. After 6hrs = dead engagement.
